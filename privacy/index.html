MagiScan Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy covers MagiScan, the independent Magic: The Gathering card scanner and collection app provided by Anul Agarwal. It applies to the iOS and Android app identified by com.momo.magiscan.

Your collection

Saved cards, quantities, folders, favorites, questionnaire preferences and scan images are stored locally by the app. MagiScan does not provide a collection account or collection cloud-sync service. Your device's operating system may include local app data in backups according to your device settings.

You can remove cards and folders in the app, clear local collection data in Settings, or uninstall the app. CSV exports and card details are shared only when you choose an export or sharing action. The destination you select then handles the shared file or text under its own policies.

Card photos and OpenAI

Scanning uses your camera permission to photograph a card. Before transmission, you can review and crop the photo. Selecting Send to OpenAI sends the cropped photo through our MagiScan scanning proxy to OpenAI to read the card's identifying information, such as its name, set, collector number, printed language and visible slab details. Recognition is a suggestion that you should review.

The proxy processes the photo without storing the photo. After a successful scan, the cropped scan image is saved locally with your collection. OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs under its own API data-handling and abuse-monitoring rules. Processing by OpenAI is separate from local storage. See OpenAI's API data controls and OpenAI's privacy policy.

Include only the card. Keep people, faces, addresses, payment details and other personal information out of photos. You can decline transmission by retaking the photo, closing the camera or using catalog search instead. Camera permission can be withdrawn in your device settings. Closing the camera or withdrawing permission prevents new photo transmissions; it does not recall data already sent to a provider.

Catalog and price services

Catalog searches, printing identification and raw price-guide data use Scryfall. Scryfall receives the public card search or printing information needed for the request and the network information associated with a connection. Graded price-guide requests go through the MagiScan proxy to PriceCharting using card and set names. Card photos are not sent to Scryfall or PriceCharting. These providers manage their services under their own policies: Scryfall privacy and PriceCharting privacy.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple or Google and managed in the app through RevenueCat. The store and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information, transaction identifiers and an app-user identifier to provide billing, restore purchases and determine premium access. MagiScan does not receive your payment-card details. See RevenueCat privacy, Apple privacy and Google privacy.

Removing local collection data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the store account used for purchase.

Service operation and retention

Our proxy and hosting services may process request metadata such as an IP address, request time, requested endpoint, response status and technical device or connection information to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. This is distinct from storing card photos. We do not sell your personal information or use card photos for advertising.

Local collection data remains until you remove it or remove the app, subject to device backups you control. OpenAI, store, RevenueCat and catalog-service records are retained under the respective providers' policies. If you contact support, the information in your message is used to answer and resolve your request.

Children and choices

The app is a card-collection tool and does not solicit personal information from children. Do not include personal information about a child in a scan. If you believe personal information was provided improperly, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

You can use catalog browsing without sending a photo. Questionnaire answers are optional. You can choose your app language in Settings and review how scanning works there. Device permission controls and the app's data-removal controls remain available after onboarding.

Security and changes

Network services use HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when the app or its services change; the effective date identifies the current policy.
