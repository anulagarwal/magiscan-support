MagiScan Support

Help for MTG Card Scanner: MagiScan, the independent Magic: The Gathering card scanner and collection app by Anul Agarwal. App identifier: com.momo.magiscan.

Contact us

Email kolagames07@gmail.com with your device model, app version and a description of the problem. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords or private information.

Scanning and card information

Use a clear, well-lit card photo and review the crop before selecting Send to OpenAI. Include only the card. Confirm the identified set, collector number and foil finish; different printings have different guide estimates. Catalog browsing works without sending a photo. Guide prices are estimates and are not guaranteed sale proceeds or grading results.

Collection and language

Collection data and saved scan images are stored on your device. You can manage quantities, folders and your Wish List in the app. Use CSV export to share a collection report. Choose a language in Settings or follow your device language. Removing the app may remove local collection data, subject to device backups.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the store account used to buy premium. To manage or cancel an iOS subscription, use Apple subscription settings. On Android, use Google Play subscription settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing local collection data does not cancel subscriptions. Stores manage billing and refund decisions.

Privacy and terms

Read the MagiScan Privacy Policy for local storage, photo recognition providers and your choices. The iOS app uses the standard Apple End User License Agreement where applicable.
